Societies Registration

Registered Non-Profit Society

Registered under the Societies Registration Act XXI of 1860 as Aspire – A Society for the Promotion of Inclusive and Relevant Education.

🏛️

Section 80G Approval

Income Tax Exemption

Donations made to ASPIRE are eligible for 50% tax deduction under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act, 1961.

FCRA Registration

Independent Governance & Ethics

ASPIRE is governed by an eminent Board of Educationists chaired by Dr. N.V. Varghese (former Vice Chancellor, NIEPA). Financial audits are performed by independent certified chartered accountants. For specific queries regarding statutory certificates, email info@aspire-india.org.

ASPIRE (A Society for the Promotion of Inclusive and Relevant Education) is dedicated to ensuring quality education for every child in the most remote areas of India.
