Governance Rooted in Public Scholarship & Integrity.

ASPIRE is guided by an eminent Governing Body and Advisory Board comprising internationally renowned education planners, public policy scholars, child rights champions, and institutional administrators.

Dr. N.V. Varghese

Chairperson, Governing Body

Former Vice Chancellor of the National Institute of Educational Planning and Administration (NIEPA), New Delhi, and Founding Director of CPRHE. Former Head of Governance and Management in Education at IIEP / UNESCO (Paris). Decades of distinguished global leadership in educational planning.

Dr. Shantha Sinha

Member, Advisory Board

Ramon Magsaysay Awardee and Padma Shri recipient. Former first Chairperson of the National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR), Government of India. Founder of MV Foundation and pioneer of the Child Labour Free Zone methodology.

Dayaram

Founder & Executive Secretary

Founder of ASPIRE India. Former Education Head at Aga Khan Foundation (India) and Chief Consultant for 7 years in Alternative Schooling under DPEP / Sarva Shiksha Abhiyan (SSA), Ministry of Education, Government of India. M.Phil in Sociology from JNU.

FK

Dr. Farida Abdulla Khan

Governing Body Member

Former Professor at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, and former Member of the National Commission for Minorities (NCM). Renowned authority on developmental psychology, inclusive education, and child rights.

SM

Prof. Shyam B Menon

Member, Advisory Board

Founding Vice Chancellor of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar University Delhi (AUD) and senior Professor at the Central Institute of Education (CIE), University of Delhi. Renowned educational philosopher and policy thinker.

DJ

Dr. Dhir Jhingran

Member, Advisory Board

Founder Director of the Language and Learning Foundation (LLF). Former IAS officer, former Principal Secretary of Education, and international specialist in foundational language acquisition and early learning.

BA

Binoy Acharya

Governing Body Member

Director of UNNATI — Organisation for Development Education. Expert in democratic local governance, social audit frameworks, and civil society accountability across western and central India.

Dr. Madhumita Bandyopadhyay

Governing Body Member

Professor at the Department of School and Non-Formal Education, NIEPA, New Delhi. Author of major national research papers on secondary education access, gender equity, and tribal education.

SV

Shankar Venkateswaran

Member, Advisory Board

Former Chief of Tata Sustainability Group, former Executive Director at PwC India, and co-founder of the Sustainability Advisory Council. Specialist in corporate citizenship and impact alignment.

RM

Prof. Rajeev Malhotra

Member, Advisory Board

Professor of Public Policy at O.P. Jindal Global University. Former Economic Advisor to the Ministry of Finance and Planning Commission of India. Author of landmark treatises on human rights indicators.

SJ

Dr. Swati Jha

Governing Body Member

Public health and child development specialist. Guides ASPIRE’s convergence initiatives connecting Anganwadis, health sub-centres, and primary schools for holistic child health.

DK

Danger Kalyan Vajsurbhai

Governing Body Member

Grassroots community leader and rural education activist. Dedicated to pastoral and marginalized tribal community rights and educational inclusion.

UR

Dr. N. Uppender Reddy

Governing Body Member

Former Professor and Head of the Curriculum and Textbooks Department at SCERT Telangana. Leading authority on constructivist pedagogy, bilingual reader design, and continuous comprehensive evaluation (CCE).

PS

Prof. Pushpendra Kumar Singh

Governing Body Member

Professor at the Tata Institute of Social Sciences (TISS), Patna Centre. Scholar of agrarian migration, rural labor dynamics, and socio-economic exclusion.

OK

Dr. Om Prakash Kulhari

Governing Body Member

Senior specialist in rural livelihoods, child rights education, and natural resource governance. Guides ASPIRE’s community convergence programs in tribal districts.
